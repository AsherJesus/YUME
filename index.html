DIGITAL AUDIO ARCHIVE / INSTRUCTIONS

使い方 / HOW TO USE

01 / GET YOUR MUSIC

Before using YUME AUDIO SYSTEM, make sure you have music files saved on your device.

You can use MP3, WAV, OGG and other audio files supported by your browser.

Download music from legitimate sources that allow downloads, or use audio files you already own.

YUME AUDIO SYSTEM does not download songs from YouTube or streaming services.

02 / LOAD YOUR MUSIC

Select 曲を選ぶ / LOAD from the main menu.

Your device's file selection window will appear.

Choose one song or select multiple audio files to create your playlist.

Your music will begin playing after it loads.

03 / MUSIC CONTROLS

曲を選ぶ / LOAD — Select audio files.

再生する / PLAY — Start or resume music.

一時停止 / PAUSE — Pause your song.

次の曲 / NEXT — Skip to the next track.

使い方 / GUIDE — Open these instructions.

04 / AUDIO VISUALIZER

The green frequency bars move in response to the music you're playing.

The particle display creates a dreamy digital atmosphere.

When music is stopped, the spectrum continues with a decorative idle animation.

05 / IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Your selected audio files are played locally in your browser.

This music player does not upload your selected songs to a server.

Refreshing or closing the page clears your playlist.

Simply select LOAD again to continue listening.

音楽を楽しんでください。 ENJOY YOUR MUSIC.
